添削例（英検2級・英作文）

出題：Do you think it is good for high school students to have a part-time job?（意見論述・80〜100語）

STEP 1 届いた答案（原文のまま）
I think students should have a part-time job. I have two reasons. First, they can learn how important money is. If they work hard, they will get money and they can buy what they want to buy. Second, students can experience many things. For example, talking with people who is older than them. It is difficult to experience in school life. Because of these reasons, working is a good experience for students, so I think students should have a part-time job.
81語
STEP 2 観点ごとの採点
観点 素点
内容3 / 4
構成3 / 4
語彙3 / 4
文法2 / 4
合計11 / 16
STEP 3 直した箇所と、その理由（抜粋2件）
talking with people who is older than them talking with people who are older than them

先行詞の people は複数なので、関係代名詞のあとの動詞は are です。文の組み立て自体は正しいので、直すのはこの一致だけで大丈夫です。

It is difficult to experience in school life. This is something they cannot easily experience in school life.

この It が何を指すのか、読み手には伝わりません。さらに experience の目的語も抜けています。前の文の内容を This で受けて、something を目的語に立てると意味が通ります。

STEP 4 書き直した答案
I think it is good for students to have a part-time job. I have two reasons. First, students can learn how important money is. When they earn money by themselves, they start to think carefully before they spend it. Second, a part-time job gives students chances to talk with different kinds of people, such as customers and older workers. This is something they cannot easily experience in school life. For these two reasons, I think having a part-time job is a good experience for students.
85語
返却時のコメント 文法2点の失点は、どちらも「書いた直後に一度読み返せば気づける」種類のミスです。提出前に30秒だけ音読する習慣をつけましょう。それだけで文法は3点に乗ります。内容面では、2つ目の理由が「many things」のまま終わっているのがもったいない点です。For example で例を出したら、「それが自分にとって何になるのか」まで1文足すと、内容も4点が狙えます。次回は同じテーマで、この2点だけ意識して書き直してみてください。
